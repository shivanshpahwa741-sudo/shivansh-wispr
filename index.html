What I did when my own fix did not work.

At reform the founder told me I was not updating the CRM often enough for customer learning to compound. He was right about the gap, so I fixed it properly. The records improved and sales did not move. When I looked at where orders were actually coming from, nearly all of them traced back to a face-to-face conversation, not to anything in the pipeline. I told him, he agreed, and I rebuilt my weeks around in-person visits with the CRM as the record rather than the strategy.

I would rather find out I was solving the wrong problem in week two than defend the fix for a month. That is also how I read a dashboard: the chart that confirms what everyone already believes is usually the least useful one on the page.

Act on the feedback fully first, then let the results say what to change next.
